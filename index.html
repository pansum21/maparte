The Ultimate Paper & Framing Guide for Map Art

Printing digital map art gives you the freedom to choose your favorite museum-grade paper stock and bespoke frame moulding without paying inflated retail markups:

Recommended Paper Stocks

  • Enhanced Matte (210–250 gsm): The #1 recommendation for minimalist cartography. Absorbs light without reflection, delivering rich deep blacks and subtle pastel hues.
  • Satin / Luster (240–260 gsm): Offers a slight gentle sheen that highlights water contours and coastal coastlines without heavy glare.
  • 100% Cotton Rag (300+ gsm): Archival museum-grade paper that lasts over 100 years without yellowing. Ideal for wedding and milestone gifts.

Selecting the Right Frame Finish

  • Classic Matte Black: Creates high contrast and clean architectural lines. Complements Asphalt, Carrara, and Noir themes in modern apartments.
  • Natural Scandinavian Oak: Warm solid wood moulding that adds organic richness to neutral, Japandi, and boho living rooms. Pairs beautifully with Terracotta.
  • Modern Museum White: Crisp gallery aesthetic that seamlessly integrates into bright, airy, coastal, and minimalist interior spaces.
