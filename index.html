Frequently asked questions

How do I start a map?

Search for a place in the location field or enter coordinates, then adjust the map area and choose a layout. Place-name search and map tiles rely on external OpenStreetMap-based services.

Can I make a poster for a small town or village?

You can search by place name or use coordinates to move the map to a specific area. Available map detail depends on the underlying map data at that location.

What can I customize?

Choose a theme and layout, change supported map layers, adjust borders, add location labels and a tagline, and tune text styling.

Can I add my own markers or a route?

The Markers panel includes built-in and emoji choices and supports custom icon uploads. The Routes panel accepts GPX tracks and provides route and endpoint styling.

Which file formats can I download?

Open the Download panel to see the formats and output settings currently available for your design. Resolution and file details depend on the selected output and poster dimensions.

Does MAPARTE use my device location automatically?

No. Device location is requested only when you choose the location control. If you use place search or reverse geocoding, the relevant location query is sent to the map-search service. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Why is OpenStreetMap credited on the map?

MAPARTE uses OpenStreetMap data and map services. The credit and license link need to remain visible with the map or exported artwork.

How do I prepare a design for printing?

Choose the intended physical dimensions before exporting. Check the selected file pixel dimensions and effective resolution with your print provider, and confirm their bleed, color, and paper requirements.
