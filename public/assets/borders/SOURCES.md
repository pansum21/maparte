- Collection record and related floral frame files: https://freesvg.org/flourish-decorative-frame
- Flourish Frame 10 record: https://freesvg.org/flourish-frame-10
- Golden Picture Frame record (related FloralFrame32): https://freesvg.org/golden-frame-square
- FreeSVG public-domain license notes: https://freesvg.org/pages/integrations

These are SVG vectors, so their edge shapes are not limited by a fixed raster pixel size. They are fitted to the poster's current proportions and clipped to the outer mat ring so they cannot cover the map opening. The decorative collection is a first sourced set, not a claim that 50 distinct frames are already present.

- Local file: `color-corner-oc-301668.svg`
- Adaptation: the source SVG's viewBox is cropped to the upper-right corner; the vector artwork is otherwise retained. The app rotates it for the other corners.
