Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about creating your custom 'Where We Met' print.

Can I map multiple cities or road trips?

Yes! You can place multiple custom markers across points of interest, or upload a GPX track of your road trip, sailing voyage, or backpacking route.

Can I use custom languages for the city name?

Yes, MAPARTE supports Japanese (漢字), Cyrillic, Greek, Hindi (देवनागरी), and all Latin character sets for foreign city names.

What shapes work best for travel posters?

The Classic Circle (Scandinavian Orb) and Modern Arch are top choices for international travel wall art.

How do I create a matching set of travel prints?

You can use the same theme (e.g. Classic Carrara or Gaia Terracotta) across 3 different cities for a unified triptych look.
