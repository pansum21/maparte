Helpful to know

Frequently asked questions.

What places can I map?

Search by place name or enter coordinates to move the map to a specific location.

Can I change the map and border style?

Yes. Choose a theme and supported map layers, set a layout, and adjust the border.

Can I add labels, markers or a route?

Add location labels and a tagline, place supported markers, or import a GPX track.

Can I make a phone wallpaper?

Choose a wallpaper layout, preview the digital design, and download the available map file.

Do you ship printed maps?

MAPARTE provides digital map designs and downloads. We do not print or ship physical products.

How can I use my downloaded map?

Use it as a phone or screen wallpaper, or arrange your own print if you want a physical copy.
