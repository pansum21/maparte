Helpful to know

Frequently asked questions.

What places can I map?

Search by place name or enter coordinates to move the map to a specific location.

Can I change the map and border style?

Yes. Choose a theme and supported map layers, set a layout, and adjust the border.

Can I add labels, markers or a route?

Add location labels and a tagline, place supported markers, or import a GPX track.

Can I make a phone wallpaper?

Choose a wallpaper layout in the editor, preview the design, and prepare it for export.
