# Privacy Policy for MAPARTE

**Last updated:** October 16, 2026

MAPARTE ("we", "our", or "us") provides a web-based map poster and wallpaper creator at [maparte.app](https://maparte.app). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data you may share with us.

---

## 1. Overview

MAPARTE runs primarily inside your web browser. Creating map posters does not require you to register, sign up, or provide personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or billing details.

---

## 2. Information We Process

### 2.1 Location Data
When you use features like "My Location" or enter a city name:
- Your coordinates and location searches are used exclusively in real time to center the map and fetch map tiles from OpenStreetMap / OpenFreeMap via Nominatim geocoding.
- We do not store or track your real-time GPS coordinates on any central database.

### 2.2 Local Storage
We use browser `localStorage` solely to save your local design preferences (such as selected color themes, custom coordinates, font choices, and export settings) so they persist across your sessions on the same device. This information never leaves your browser.

### 2.3 Analytics (Google Analytics 4)
We may use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors interact with the site, with IP anonymization enabled. GA4 collects pseudonymous device and usage telemetry (such as page views and browser types).

---

## 3. Advertising & Cookies (Google AdSense)

To keep MAPARTE free for everyone, we display advertisements provided by **Google AdSense**.

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
- **Opting Out:** You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/) or by visiting [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).
- In regions subject to GDPR (European Economic Area, UK, and Switzerland), we implement Google Consent Mode v2 to ensure advertising cookies are only activated with your consent.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

MAPARTE relies on trusted third-party providers for mapping data and hosting:
- **OpenStreetMap & OpenFreeMap:** Map data and vector tiles.
- **Nominatim:** Geocoding and reverse geocoding services.
- **Hosting / CDN:** Vercel / Cloudflare for fast, secure content delivery.

These providers process standard web request logs (such as IP addresses and User-Agent headers) in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

---

## 5. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Under applicable data protection laws (including GDPR and CCPA), you have the right to:
- Access, update, or delete your stored data (you can clear your browser cookies and `localStorage` at any time).
- Withdraw consent for analytics or personalized advertising via our cookie consent controls.
