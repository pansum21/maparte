# Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026. MapArte respects your privacy and ensures your location search queries are never sold to third parties.

## 1. Information We Collect
We collect minimal information necessary to deliver our services: map location preferences, email addresses provided for order delivery, and anonymous analytics via Google Analytics to optimize site performance.

## 2. Payment Processing
We do not store your credit card or sensitive financial information. All cross-border transactions are securely handled by **Dodo Payments** (Merchant of Record) in compliance with PCI-DSS standards.

## 3. Cookies & Analytics
We use standard cookies for site functionality and aggregated traffic measurement (Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster, Pinterest tag). You can manage cookie preferences via your browser settings.

## 4. Advertising & Google AdSense
To support free digital creation, we may display advertisements via Google AdSense. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on prior visits. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. In GDPR regions, Google Consent Mode v2 ensures cookies are activated only with user consent.
