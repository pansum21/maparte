# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains what the current MAPARTE website does with information when you use the map editor. MAPARTE is a browser-based design tool. It does not currently offer user accounts or paid checkout. Third-party services linked below operate under their own privacy terms.

## Information kept in your browser

The editor uses browser storage for preferences and working data. Depending on the features you use, this can include recent places and coordinates, cached map searches, selected themes and layouts, export preferences, custom marker settings and a small local diagnostics backlog. The current diagnostics code keeps that backlog in the browser rather than sending it to a diagnostics server. Older values from earlier versions may remain in your browser until you clear this site’s data. You can clear recent places in the editor settings or clear MAPARTE site data in your browser.

## Place searches, maps and location

When you submit a place search, the search text is sent to OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim geocoding service so it can return matching places. Selecting a location may send its coordinates for reverse geocoding. Search suggestions are requested after you submit a search, rather than on every keystroke. The map also requests map tiles and related map data from OpenFreeMap as you view and move around it. Those services receive the network information needed to answer the request and are governed by their own policies.

Your device location is used only if you choose the location control and approve your browser’s location prompt. You can deny or later revoke that permission in your browser settings.

## Images and route files

The current editor reads GPX route files and preview images in your browser to use them in the map design. It does not upload those files to a MAPARTE file-storage service. Custom marker images may be converted to browser data and saved in your site storage so they remain available on that device. Do not use a shared device if you want that local data to remain private; remove the saved marker or clear site data when finished.

## Analytics, advertising and performance

The website includes Google Analytics, Google AdSense, Ahrefs Analytics, Vercel Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights. These services may use cookies or similar technologies and may receive technical information such as device/browser details, page activity, approximate network location and performance measurements. Google Consent Mode is configured with analytics and advertising storage denied by default for the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and granted defaults for other configured regions. Where Google's Funding Choices consent interface is available, you can use it to manage the choices it presents. If that interface is unavailable, use the controls offered by your browser and the providers.

Google, Ahrefs and Vercel process information under their own privacy policies. You can also use browser controls to block or clear cookies and site storage; doing so may affect parts of the site.

## Accounts, payments and emails

The current site does not provide account registration or a paid checkout. The free phone-wallpaper download does not require payment. MAPARTE does not send an email address or payment details to a payment processor through the current site. If paid checkout or accounts are introduced, this policy will be updated before those features are offered.

If you contact [support@maparte.com](mailto:support@maparte.com), MAPARTE receives the information you choose to include in your message and uses it to respond to your request.

## Your choices and questions

You can avoid submitting a place search, deny device-location permission, manage browser storage and cookies, and use available consent controls. To ask about this policy or a privacy request, email [support@maparte.com](mailto:support@maparte.com). The site does not list a separate legal entity or postal address; none is inferred here.

## Policy updates

This policy may change when the website’s features, providers or legal requirements change. The updated date at the top indicates the latest version published here.
